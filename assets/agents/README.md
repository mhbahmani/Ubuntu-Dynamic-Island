# Harness icons

These SVGs identify the third-party harness in session rows. They are bundled
locally without changing their paths, proportions or colors; no remote images
are fetched by the extension.

- `claude.svg`: Anthropic's Claude mark, from https://claude.com/icon.svg
- `codex.svg`: OpenAI's Blossom icon, from https://developers.openai.com/favicon.svg
- `opencode.svg`: OpenCode's square logo for dark backgrounds, from
  https://opencode.ai/brand/files/opencode-logo-dark-square.svg

The marks belong to Anthropic, OpenAI and Anomaly respectively. They identify
supported integrations and do not imply endorsement. Brand references:
https://openai.com/brand/ and https://opencode.ai/brand.

The remaining `*-symbolic.svg` files are Ubuntu Island's state indicators.
